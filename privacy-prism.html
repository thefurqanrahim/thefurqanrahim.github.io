Privacy Policy for Prism Strike

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BrightSpark Studio handles information in the mobile game Prism Strike.

1. Information We Collect

Prism Strike does not require account registration and does not directly collect your name, email, or personal files. Your wave progress, high scores, and sound preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), approximate location, and app interaction data to serve relevant ads in accordance with regional consent regulations (GDPR / US State Privacy Laws).

Learn more: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Prism Strike is intended for a general audience aged 18 and older and is not directed at children under 13.
